The Earth layer is generated by `tools/data/build_world.py` from these sources. Run `python3 tools/data/fetch.py` and then `python3 tools/data/build_world.py` to rebuild it. The generator needs numpy and Pillow.

## Choices the generator makes

- **Nations:** every sovereign country in Natural Earth's 1:50m set, except Antarctica.
  - Dependencies belong to their sovereign, e.g. Greenland to Denmark and Puerto Rico to the United States.
  - European microstates are folded into the surrounding country. These are Vatican City and San Marino (Italy), Monaco (France), Andorra (Spain) and Liechtenstein (Switzerland).
  - Island microstates form two groups, as Terra Invicta does for small neighbours: the Eastern Caribbean States and the Pacific Island States.
  - Disputed areas follow de facto administration: Somaliland → Somalia, Northern Cyprus → Cyprus, Western Sahara → Morocco, Siachen → India. Kosovo, Palestine and Taiwan are their own nations.
- **Regions:** each nation's provinces are clustered into regions with population-weighted k-means. The number of regions grows with population and land area, for about 360 in total. A region is named after its most populous province.
- **Breakaway homelands** (`tools/data/breakaways.json`, from `build_breakaways.py`): each of Terra Invicta's breakaway nations gets one more region in its parent, so it can secede without taking the capital. Its capital's province (snapped from Terra Invicta's rough map coordinates, the one nearest its centre winning a clash) and the parent's capital province are pinned as cluster centres, and the k-means fills in around them. That gives 478 regions. A breakaway's region is named after its capital city (Glasgow, Barcelona, Dallas), or after its province if another region of the nation already has that name. Some regions are named by hand in `build_world.py`: breakaway homelands in `REGION_NAMES` (North West Territories, St. John's) and other regions in `REGION_RENAMES` (Canada's provinces by their capitals: Victoria, Regina, Winnipeg, Ottawa, Halifax). Canada's capital is pinned to Ontario, since Ottawa's point falls in Québec on our raster.
- **Region population** splits the national total by the population of the region's cities, plus a small area term.

## Claims — `tools/data/build_claims.py`

Run order: `build_breakaways.py`, `build_world.py`, `build_claims.py`, then `build_research.py`. Changing the regions means old saves can't be loaded (`src/sim/save.ts`).

Claims come from Terra Invicta's TIBilateralTemplate (relationType "Claim"), with TIRegionTemplate, TIMapRegionTemplate (region latitude and longitude) and TINationTemplate to match its map to ours (`src/content/claims.json`). Run it before `build_research.py`, which keeps the projects that unlock claims. How the matching works is in `docs/formulas.md` ("Claims").

## Solar System — `tools/data/build_solar.py`

The bodies and their orbits come from Terra Invicta's TISpaceBodyTemplate, in the game's install or the same `Pzixel/terra-invicta-techtree-update` repository; the script's docstring has the command. It keeps every body with a full set of orbital elements (485 bodies): the Sun, the planets, their moons, the 125 asteroidal moons (Terra Invicta's class for small irregular moons such as Phobos, Deimos and most of the giants' moons, plus the small moons of Pluto, Haumea and Quaoar), the dwarf planets, asteroids and comets, with names, radii, masses and Keplerian elements. Moons' semi-major axes are converted from km to AU; for Jupiter's moons the argument of periapsis and mean anomaly are worked out from the longitude of periapsis and mean longitude the game gives. Fourteen bodies whose elements the game leaves incomplete are filled in by `tools/data/solar_overrides.json` (each entry names its source): the comets Pons-Winnecke and Lovas 2 and eight distant objects from the JPL Small-Body Database (retrieved 2026-10-29), Styx and Kerberos from JPL's Pluto satellite elements (Brozović & Jacobson 2024), Hiʻiaka from Ragozzine & Brown 2009 (the fit the game's Namaka comes from) and Weywot from Proudfoot et al. 2025. Masses the game lacks for them are estimates from its diameters, at 1.0 g/cm³ for trans-Neptunian objects and 0.5 for comet nuclei, except Gǃkúnǁʼhòmdímà's (measured, Grundy et al. 2019). The game's "512381" is a typo for (528381) 2008 ST291, whose orbit and size it gives. Left out: the hypothetical Planet Nine and the 9 bodies whose orbits are not yet published in full (Dysnomia, Vanth, Ilmarë, Actaea, Gǃòʼé ǃHú, Xiangliu, Tinia, Linus and Menoetius). Some dwarf-planet names are Terra Invicta's own for unnamed objects.

## Surface maps of other bodies — `tools/data/build_body_maps.py`

`public/map/mercury.png`, `luna.png`, `mars.png`, `ceres.png` and `venus.png` (each 1024 × 512, equirectangular, −180° to 180° east like `earth.png`) are built by `python3 -I tools/data/build_body_maps.py [body …]` from public-domain sources that `fetch.py` downloads (about 2.2 GB in all, nearly all of it Mercury's and Mars's mosaics):

| Body | Albedo and colour | Relief |
|---|---|---|
| Mercury | [MESSENGER MDIS global colour mosaic, 665 m/px](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mercury_messenger_mdis_global_color_mosaic_665m) (USGS; Denevi et al.; 1000, 750 and 430 nm as red, green and blue) | [USGS global DEM from MESSENGER stereo, 665 m/px](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mercury_messenger_global_dem_665m) (Becker et al.) |
| Luna | LRO LROC WAC colour map, 4096 × 2048, from NASA SVS's [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720) | LOLA heights at 16 px/degree, same kit |
| Mars | [Viking colour mosaic, 925 m/px](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_global_color_mosaic_925m) (USGS) | MGS MOLA MEGDR at 16 px/degree (PDS Geosciences, `megt90n000eb.img`) |
| Ceres | Dawn Framing Camera global mosaic at 20 px/degree (DLR, via USGS; greyscale) | the mosaic's own sunlit shading |
| Venus | made up: its cloud tops after Mariner 10's view (NASA/JPL-Caltech PIA23791) | — |

Mercury's and the Moon's mosaics are photometrically flat, so their relief is a hillshade of the DEM lit from the north-west, mixed into the brightness; Mars's Viking mosaic is made of sunlit images and gets a little of MOLA's on top; Ceres's has only its own. Colours are pulled back from the mosaics' stretch to each body's real tones, keeping the regional differences (Mercury's bluish low-reflectance material and warmer smooth plains, the Moon's bluish maria). Gaps are filled from around them; where a mosaic is poor near a pole (Mercury north of 78° and south of 72°S, Ceres beyond 80°) brightness and colour fade to the average, and Mars's messy caps become clean ice beyond 81°N and 84°S. Then each goes onto a hand-picked palette with ordered dithering, as for the Earth. Venus's map is a cream-white globe with a soft, mottled peach haze across the low latitudes, white wisps sweeping poleward in chevrons, a faint greyer collar and bright polar hoods, made from fixed-seed value noise.

The bodies turn to the IAU's rotational elements (Archinal et al. 2018; `src/app/solar/bodyFrames.ts`), so the maps' longitudes face the right way: Mercury, Mars (the 2009 elements, periodic terms left out) and Ceres (Dawn's). The Moon keeps its near side towards the Earth as it goes round. Venus turns with its cloud tops, westward in about four days, not its surface.

## Research — `tools/data/build_research.py`

Techs and projects come from Terra Invicta's own template files and English localization: TITechTemplate, TIProjectTemplate and TIEffectTemplate. These are published with the community tech tree site ([pzixel.github.io/terra-invicta-techtree-update](https://pzixel.github.io/terra-invicta-techtree-update), repository `Pzixel/terra-invicta-techtree-update`, folder `public/gamefiles/stable`). The script's docstring has the commands.

- **Techs** (`src/content/techs.json`): names, categories, research costs, prerequisites, summaries, and the effects our simulation applies.
- **Projects** (`src/content/projects.json`): names (faction names swapped for ours), categories, costs, prerequisites, faction restrictions, unlock and availability chances, summaries, one-time grants and effects. Effects are translated into our own effect kinds. Which projects are kept is in `docs/formulas.md` ("Research").
